## Compressed text: Platform onboarding

Welcome to platform onboarding. All new employees sign in through Single Sign-On (SSO); after first login, SSO remembers your session for the rest of the day. Every account requires Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA); set up MFA on your first day. User accounts are auto-provisioned from the HR system through System for Cross-domain Identity Management (SCIM), so there is no manual signup. Contact the help desk if SSO fails; if MFA is lost, it can reset MFA. Help-desk tickets have a one-business-day Service Level Agreement (SLA).
