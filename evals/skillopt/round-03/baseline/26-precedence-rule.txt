## Compressed text: On-call policy

On-call policy. The primary on-call engineer acknowledges every page within 15 minutes; otherwise, the page automatically escalates to the secondary on-call engineer. The runbook is the source of truth: if it and the monitoring dashboard disagree on incident severity, follow the runbook, not the dashboard. During a Sev-1 incident, the incident commander has final authority and may override standard change-freeze rules; outside an active incident, those rules always apply and no one may override them. After every incident, write a blameless postmortem within three business days.
