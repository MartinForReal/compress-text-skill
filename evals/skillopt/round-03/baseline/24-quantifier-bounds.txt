## Compressed text: Password requirements for the account system

Account-system passwords must be 12-64 characters; shorter than 12 is rejected at sign-up. They must include at least one uppercase letter and at least one digit, and must not reuse any of the user's last 5 passwords. Accounts lock after exactly 5 consecutive failed login attempts and stay locked at least 30 minutes. Password-reset links are valid only 15 minutes after issuance.
