## Compressed text: On-call policy

On-call policy. Pages go first to the primary on-call engineer, who acknowledges every page within 15 minutes; otherwise, the page automatically escalates to the secondary on-call engineer. Severity follows the runbook, the source of truth, over the monitoring dashboard when they disagree. During a Sev-1 incident, the incident commander has final authority and may override standard change-freeze rules. Outside an active incident, change-freeze rules always apply and no one may override them. After every incident, write a blameless postmortem within three business days.
