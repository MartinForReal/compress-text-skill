## Compressed text: Password requirements

Password rules:
- Passwords must be 12-64 characters; anything under 12 is rejected at sign-up.
- Each password must include at least one uppercase letter and at least one digit, and must not reuse any of the user's last 5 passwords.
- Accounts lock after exactly 5 consecutive failed login attempts and stay locked for at least 30 minutes.
- Password reset links are valid only for 15 minutes after issuance.
