# Partner launch handoff

Every partner launch needs a named launch owner and dated checklist.

## API launch
- Send sandbox credentials.
- Verify the OAuth redirect URI.
- Publish the API changelog.
- Rotate the test secret after sandbox validation.

## Marketing launch
- Confirm approved logo placement.
- Get a customer quote.
- Set campaign UTM parameters.
- Schedule social posts.

Do not bundle API checklist items into the marketing handoff.
