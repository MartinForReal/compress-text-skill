# Operations quick guide

Use the internal portal for every request; it creates the audit trail.

## Production incidents
1. Set the severity.
2. If Sev-1 or Sev-2, page the on-call engineer.
3. Open an incident channel.
4. Write the timeline and postmortem within three business days.

## Vendor access
1. In the portal, submit the business justification, manager approval, and expiry date.
2. Send the request to Security for review.
3. Remove access when the contract ends.
