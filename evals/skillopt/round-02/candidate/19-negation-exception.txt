## Compressed text: Access policy for production logs

Production log access policy:
- Engineers may read production logs.
- Engineers must never export logs to personal devices or personal cloud accounts.
- Contractors may read logs only when they have signed the current NDA and a manager has granted temporary, time-boxed access; otherwise they may not read them.
- No one may disable audit logging under any circumstances, even briefly for debugging.
