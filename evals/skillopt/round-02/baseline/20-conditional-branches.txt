## Compressed text: Deployment rules

Deployment rules:
- Green build: deploy to staging automatically.
- Red build: do not deploy; page the on-call engineer.
- Production deploys require two approvals from senior engineers before release.
- Never deploy to production on a Friday or during a release freeze.
- If a production deploy fails, roll back immediately and open an incident ticket.
