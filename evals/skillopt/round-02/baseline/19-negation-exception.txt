## Compressed text: Access policy for production logs

Production log access policy:
- All engineers may read production logs as part of normal daily work.
- Engineers must never export production logs to personal devices or personal cloud accounts.
- Contractors may not read production logs unless they have signed the current NDA and a manager has granted temporary, time-boxed access.
- No one may disable audit logging under any circumstances, even briefly for debugging.
